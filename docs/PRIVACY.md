# Privacy Policy

Bear Media. Last changed 17 October 2026.

## What the developer collects

Nothing. There's no account to make, nothing is measured or tracked, and the app sends nothing to its developer.

## What stays on this computer

Your library, playlists, favourites, play counts, listening time, what you've watched, your settings and your sign-ins are kept on this computer, in your library's folder and the app's own settings folder. Deleting those deletes them.

## What leaves this computer

Only what it takes to do what you ask. A search, a song or video played or downloaded, lyrics and covers looked up, and a plugin's lists and films are each asked of the service or plugin that has them, which sees what was asked for and your internet address, as it would in a web browser. A film played or downloaded from a torrent is fetched from, and sent on to, other people who have it, who can see your internet address, as with any torrent program. If you connect Spotify or Last.fm, the app reads your playlists and lists from them, and changes nothing there. If you import from Stremio, the app reads what Stremio on this Mac remembers of what you've watched (on this Mac only; Stremio isn't changed, and its sign-in isn't read), and asks your plugins for each title's details.

## Sync with your devices

Off until you switch it on. Then a device you've paired with a code can copy your library over your home network, and nothing is sent to the internet for it. A video app you've paired also sends what you've watched on it (your Watchlist, what you've watched and where you got to), which is added to your profile here, and can copy its downloaded films into your Movies folder; all of it over your home network only.

## Other people's services

Every service and plugin the app reaches has a privacy policy of its own, and what it does with a request is up to it, not to this app.
