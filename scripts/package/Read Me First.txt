Bear Media: trying it
=====================

Bear Media is a music and film app for the Mac: your own library of songs, music
videos and films, plus finding and playing new ones. This copy is for trying it out.
Everything it needs is inside the app.

You need macOS 14 (Sonoma) or newer. On a Mac with Apple's own chip (M1 and later),
macOS asks to install Rosetta the first time: say yes. It's Apple's and takes a minute.


Opening it the first time
-------------------------

1. Drag "Bear Media.app" into your Applications folder.

2. This copy isn't signed by Apple, so macOS stops it the first time. To allow it, do
   one of these:

   - Open Terminal (in Applications → Utilities), paste this line, and press Return:

         xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "/Applications/Bear Media.app"

     Then open Bear Media as usual.

   - Or open Bear Media. When macOS says it can't be opened, click Done, then go to
     System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click "Open Anyway" beside
     Bear Media. Confirm with your password.

3. While it gets ready, Bear Media shows a bear for a few seconds.

4. It asks where your library is. Click "Make a New Library": it makes one called
   "Bear Media" in your Music folder. If macOS asks whether Bear Media may use your
   Music, Movies or Downloads folder, choose Allow.

Films and series come from "plugins". Bear Media starts with four free ones (film
details, a list of channels, films in the public domain, and anime details). You can
add others in Settings → Plugins.


Taking it off your Mac
----------------------

Double-click "Uninstall Bear Media.command" in this folder. If macOS won't open it,
right-click it, choose Open, then click Open again. A copy is also inside the app: in
Terminal, run

    zsh "/Applications/Bear Media.app/Contents/Resources/Uninstall Bear Media.command"

The uninstaller shows a list and waits for you to type yes. Then it moves to the Trash:

- the app
- its settings, sign-ins and paired devices
- its caches and logs
- what macOS keeps for it
- its own records inside your library

The songs, videos and films you downloaded with it stay where they are: the Music
folder inside your library, and your Movies folder. Keep or delete them as you like.
Then drag this folder (with the uninstaller in it) to the Trash too, and empty the
Trash to finish.
